Safety & Process FAQs

How do you ensure zero cross-contamination? expand_more
Our facility features separate, isolated lines for Beef, Goat, and Chicken. Each section has independent ventilation and drainage systems to prevent any cross-contact between different meat types.
Can I trace my specific cut back to the farm? expand_more
Yes. Every package comes with a unique QR code. Scanning this will provide you with the batch origin, processing date, and inspection certificates.
What packaging materials do you use? expand_more
We use medical-grade, BPA-free vacuum films and eco-friendly insulated liners. Our outer packaging is 100% recyclable.
